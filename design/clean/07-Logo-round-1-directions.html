Direction D · wordmark idea

The cut x

No separate symbol. The final x in Inter Semibold is redrawn: one stroke stays straight, the other becomes a bolt. The x is the logo; it also works alone as an icon.

Strength: quietest and most typographic; nothing to draw beside the name.

Trade-off: the bolt is small at text sizes, so the lone x must carry the icon.
